The Hybrid Lifestyle

Written by: Christopher Paloskey

Why Hybrid Athlete Style?

When you think of an "in-shape" person, you may think of a bodybuilder like Arnold Schwarzenegger, or a crazy endurance athlete like Lance Armstrong in bycicling or Eliud Kipchogne in marathon running. I am not taking away anything from them as they showcase a non-human like athletic performance and display with some help of their rare genetics. If you aren't born with those gifts and you have tried to reach that pinnacle of your desired sport, look into a "Hybrid-Athlete" style. The great thing about a "Hybrid" lifestyle is that you get the best of both worlds and have little to no weaknesses. If you are strictly a bodybuilder, you may have troubles in mobile endurance situations, if you are strictly a marathon runner, you may have trouble and everyday tasks that require lifting a heavy object. As a young man about to start my full-time job as a United States Army Field Artillery Officer, I take priority in all forms of physical fitness to lead by example. After saying all of this, if you are doing any kind of exercise that is great! You are doing a lot more than what the other 25% of the US's population of not working out. But if you are trying to start, look into the hybrid split. Think about playing with your kids and running around your yard, or helping your friend move furniture out of his apartment. Whatever side you choose, start now!
