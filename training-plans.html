Training Plans for Hybrid Athletes

This a base for beginners and what their first month may look like. The biggest thing is to not overload yourself and go out to run 100 miles in week 1. Listen to your body and stay injury free!

Marathon Training Plan

If you're new to marathon prep, the first month is all about building a base. Start with 3-4 runs per week, two shorter, easy-paced runs (2-4 miles), one medium-length run, and one long run on the weekend that slowly increases each week (start around 5-6 miles). Your goal right now is consistency, not speed. Add 1 mile per week to your long run, and don't be afraid to walk when needed. Keep the pace slow enough to hold a conversation. A rest day and one optional cross-training day (like biking or swimming) will help prevent burnout.

Ironman Training Plan

This is a big one but you don't have to be perfect. Your first month of Ironman training should include 2 swim sessions, 2-3 bike rides, and 2-3 runs per week. Keep all sessions low-intensity. Think of each workout as "time on feet, pedals, or in the water" rather than a speed test. The swim might start around 500-800 yards, the bike at 45-60 minutes, and your run as simple as 3 miles. Sundays are great for a "brick" workout (bike followed by run) to get used to the transition. Focus on building endurance gradually and becoming comfortable across all three sports.

Hyrox Training Plan

For Hyrox, balance is everything. In your first month, train 4-5 days per week: 2 strength days (think full-body lifts like deadlifts, squats, and sled work), 2 conditioning days (intervals, bodyweight circuits, or CrossFit-style metcons), and 1-2 runs. Start with short 1-2 mile runs and mix in tempo runs or intervals later. Keep the weights manageable, you're training movement quality and work capacity, not one-rep maxes. Always allow time to recover. The key is learning how to move well under fatigue while keeping your form tight.
