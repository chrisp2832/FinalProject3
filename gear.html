Gear for Hybrid Athletes

Get what you need and if you want cheaper look for cheaper, these are just suggestions.

Running Shoes

Having the right pair of running shoes will help you stay injury-free while improving your performance. For long-distance races or hybrid competitions, comfort, cushioning, and stability are key. A good pair would be the ASICS Men's Gel-Contend 9 that go for $54.95 on Amazon!

GPS Watch

Having a GPS watch provides real-time data on your performance, allowing you to adjust your training plan as needed to stay on track for your race goals. It also helps prevent overtraining or undertraining by providing insights into your heart rate and pace. A good starter watch would be the Garmin Forerunner 245 Music that goes for $299 on Amazon!

Wetsuit

If you're training for triathlons or open water swims, a wetsuit is a must. It provides buoyancy and helps you swim faster in cold water. A wetsuit also protects you from the elements during long open-water swims. The Roka Maverick Pro II is on Amazon for $550, a little steep but not totally needed if you will be training at the local pool.

Goggles

We all know what goggles are and we do want to see when we swim right? They arent expensive and are a necessity especially in open-water. Amazon is selling the Speedo Vanquisher 2.0 Goggles for $20.

Road Bike

A road bike is essential for fast cycling in a triathlon or hybrid competition. It allows you to stay aerodynamic, increasing your speed while conserving energy for the run portion of the event all compared to your traditional BMX or Mountain Bike. This will be the big investement so a cheaper ranged bike is the Cannondale CAAD13 that goes for $1850 on Amazon.

Cross Trainer Shoes

Cross-trainer shoes are versatile, offering the support needed for weightlifting and the flexibility needed for running and agility drills. A durable, stable shoe is key for preventing injury during high-intensity workouts. I personally use the RAD-1 trainer shoes that are wonderful but if you want to start at a cheaper pair look into Nexus Knit Barefoot taht goes for $109.95 on Amazon.
